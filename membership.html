Sound • Stillness

The Resonance Room

A guided sound bath at Aaromale in Film Nagar. Singing bowls fill a candlelit room. Thirty people lie still, eyes closed, letting therapeutic vibrations move through them. No phones. No conversation. Just sound, stillness, and the rare permission to simply be present.

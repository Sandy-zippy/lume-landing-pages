What happens inside.

Two experiences every month. Each one designed. Never repeated. Always intimate.

Sound • Stillness

The Resonance Room

A guided sound bath experience at Aaromale in Film Nagar. Singing bowls fill a candlelit room. Thirty people lie still, eyes closed, letting therapeutic vibrations wash over them. No phones. No small talk. Just sound, stillness, and the rare permission to simply be present.

Facilitated by Pallavi Marshall • Aaromale, Film Nagar
Art • Investment

Chromatic Conversations

An intimate evening exploring art as a serious asset class. Forty selected guests. Wine in hand. A gallery director with 13 years across India, Europe, and MENA guiding the conversation. Not about decor. About positioning. About understanding what you're really buying when you buy art.

Elements by Nirvania • Banjara Hills
Music • Connection

Melody & Mingle

An open mic evening where every voice in the room was chosen to be there. Voices shook a little. Smiles stayed a little longer. Between lyrics and laughter, people found their people. The kind of night you reference months later when someone asks what you did last weekend.
